# Project Name: USA Regional Sales Analysis & Dashboard

**Role:** Data Analyst / BI Developer
**Tools & Technologies:** Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn, Plotly), Jupyter Notebook, Microsoft Power BI, Excel.
**Domain:** Retail / Sales Data Analytics

## 📌 Executive Summary
Spearheaded an end-to-end data analytics project to analyze a 4-year US regional sales dataset (2014–2018) for "Acme Co." Extracted actionable business insights regarding revenue drivers, geographic performance, profitability margins, and seasonal anomalies. The project culminated in a robust Power BI dashboard designed to empower executive decision-making for pricing and market expansion strategies.

## 🛠️ Key Responsibilities & Implementation

*   **Data Pipeline Pipeline & Wrangling:**
    *   Ingested and consolidated multiple raw Excel datasets representing sales, customers, and regional mapping.
    *   Performed rigorous data cleaning, handling missing budgets, correcting data types, and engineering key features like total cost, profit, and profit margins.
*   **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) in Python:**
    *   Conducted univariate and multivariate analysis to segment customers and evaluate product performance.
    *   Analyzed sales across multi-dimensional tiers (Channel, Product, Region, State) to identify concentration risks and high-margin opportunities.
    *   Executed temporal (time-series) analysis to uncover a significant revenue anomaly in early 2017 and map recurring seasonal surges (May-August).
*   **Business Intelligence & Visualization:**
    *   Exported pre-processed, clean datasets for seamless integration into Power BI.
    *   Designed an interactive, executive-level Power BI dashboard (using custom background templates) to visually track KPIs such as regional revenue dominance (led by the West Coast/California) and channel profitability.

## 📈 High-Level Business Impact & Insights Derived

*   **Geographic Optimization:** Identified that the West region drives ~35% of total sales (led by California with \$230M), highlighting a strategic need to bolster promotions in the underperforming Northeast (~20%).
*   **Channel Strategy:** Discovered that while Exports make up only 15% of volume, they maintain strong profit margins (37.9%), revealing a prime area for low-risk international growth.
*   **Pricing Consistency:** Proved that profit margins remain largely consistent (18%-60%) regardless of product unit price, validating the company's baseline pricing strategy across all tiers.
*   **Risk Mitigation:** Pinpointed outlier transactions and severe revenue drops (early 2017 drop to \$21.2M), prompting targeted investigations into market disruptions and out-of-stock events.
