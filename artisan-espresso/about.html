Our Story

From a single cup to a community

Est. 2024

Born From a Love of Coffee

Tea3 was born in 2024 from one simple belief — that a great cup of coffee can change your entire day. We started as a conversation between friends, a shared obsession with the journey of a single bean from soil to cup, and the conviction that Hyderabad deserved something truly extraordinary.

We source our beans from single-origin farms across India, Ethiopia, and Colombia, roasted fresh every week in our in-house micro-roastery. Every origin is chosen for its unique story — the altitude, the farmer, the process. Each cup is a chapter of that story.

Today, we're more than a café. We're a gathering place for creators, dreamers, and coffee lovers in Hyderabad. A place where the morning starts slower, conversations run deeper, and every corner was built to make you feel at home.

The Person Behind It

Arjun Mehta

Founder & Head Roaster
"I didn't open a café. I opened a feeling."

With a background in food science and a decade spent obsessing over espresso across Mumbai, Tokyo, and Melbourne, Arjun returned to Hyderabad with one vision — to build something the city had never tasted. Every element of Tea3, from the custom drum roaster to the handmade ceramic cups, reflects his relentless attention to detail and his belief that craft is a form of love.
